# Authors and research attribution

## Mykola Fedchyk

- Original author and maintainer
- Primary investigator of the AORUS 5 SE4 fault sequence
- Designer of the diagnostic methodology, event correlation and conservative
  recovery policy implemented by this project

The initial research was conducted on physical Gigabyte AORUS 5 SE4 hardware
across Linux and Windows. It included analysis of Intel xHCI controller failure,
DMAR/IOMMU evidence, NVIDIA GSP runtime-power failures, USB-C hub topology and
live recovery behavior.

Contributions from future participants should be acknowledged through Git
history and may be added to this file when appropriate.
